# Copywriting

- Be concise. Cut filler words (actually, basically, really, quite), intro phrases (would you like to, in order to, are you sure) and unneeded articles. Keep sentences under 20 words.
- Sentence case everywhere (headings, buttons, labels). Not title case.
- Plain, conversational language at about 6th-grade level. No jargon or slang. Contractions are fine.
- Front-load: put the key information first in headings, links, lists and buttons.
- Inverted pyramid: key point first, supporting detail next, background on a separate screen.
- Avoid abbreviations and acronyms; if used, spell out on first use. Write "Apartment number", not "Apt. no."
- Limit uppercase.
- Break content into short groups with descriptive headings and bullets. Headings should make sense out of context.
- Do not use "my" or "your" in form labels; use "Email".
- Use one word per concept everywhere (cart vs bag, sign up vs register, log in vs sign in).
- Use numerals for numbers ("5", not "five"); for huge numbers mix numerals and words ("1 billion").
- Avoid full stops on short UI text unless it is a full sentence with commas; be consistent across sibling elements.
- Keep text length similar across similar elements.
- Links describe the destination. Never "click here", "learn more", "read more" alone.
- Error messages: say what happened, why, and how to fix it. Never blame the user. No "oops", "sorry", "please". Example: heading "Payment failed", body "Update your payment details and try again", button "Update payment details".
