# Perception, grouping and patterns

Paraphrased from *UI Pedia* (Pixsel Academy, 2021).

## Whitespace
- Whitespace is any empty area; it separates, groups and focuses.
- Macro: margins and gaps between blocks. Micro: line height, paragraph and list gaps (drives legibility).
- Active whitespace guides the eye; passive just aids reading.
- Amount depends on content density, style, audience and brand tone. Generous = premium; tight = informational.
- Test the balance with users.

## Proximity and grouping
- Close items read as related; proximity beats colour or shape similarity.
- Gaps between groups larger than gaps inside groups; a label sits closer to its own field.
- Do not bury an action among unrelated buttons. Keep Previous and Next together.
- Distant items get overlooked: place skip links, hints and secondary options near the main action.
- Re-check grouping at every breakpoint.
- Chunk long forms (3 groups of 4 beat 12 in one block).

## Gestalt cheat sheet
| Principle | Use |
|---|---|
| Figure-ground | Make the focus area clearly foreground |
| Similarity | Same look = same function |
| Proximity | Spacing shows grouping |
| Common region | Borders or fills group items |
| Continuity | Items on a line read as a sequence |
| Closure | Minds fill gaps; good for simple icons |
| Focal point | Most contrasting element is seen first; make it the key action |

## Hierarchy
- Order, size, colour, shape and contrast set reading order. Never rely on colour alone.

## Patterns
- Use established patterns and customise to context.
- Document patterns: problem, context, solution, examples.
- No dark patterns (trick wording, hidden opt-outs, confusing opt-in phrasing). Pre-ticked consent boxes are unlawful in the EU; leave unticked.
- Design in grayscale first.
