# Icons, illustration and shadows

Paraphrased from *UI Pedia* (Pixsel Academy, 2021).

## Icons
- Principles: clarity, readability, alignment, brevity, consistency, personality, ease of use.
- Familiar metaphors; abstract icons need labels. Pick one meaning per symbol (heart vs star vs thumb).
- Enough space between strokes; avoid fine detail at small sizes.
- Same-size container, key shapes and consistent corner radius.
- Align optically (shift play triangles, let circles overshoot).
- Test in context, at size, next to siblings.
- Solid-colour outline icons; no gradient or multi-colour icons.

## Illustration
- For concepts, onboarding, empty states, tutorials, rewards, errors, mood.
- Flat solid colours from the palette; support the message; check for ambiguity. Keep mascots consistent.

## Shadows
- One light source for all shadows.
- Higher elevation = larger, softer, lighter shadow.
- Outer for lift, inner for inset. Use the two shadow tokens only.
