# Motion and microinteractions

Paraphrased from *UI Pedia* (Pixsel Academy, 2021).

- Motion guides, informs and gives feedback; decoration is secondary.
- Types: navigation, feedback/state, loading/progress, storytelling, large page animations (rare).
- Respect reduced-motion settings; never convey information by motion alone.
- Microinteraction parts: trigger (user or system) > rules > feedback > loops/modes.
- Uses: status, confirmation, error prevention, tutorials. Examples: toggles, tab-bar icon change, button press, swipe, password-strength meter, pull-to-refresh.
- Keep short, subtle and purposeful; too many animations irritate.
- Easing: linear feels mechanical; ease-out for entering, ease-in for leaving.
- Always show progress for waits (spinner, bar, steps).
- Test on real devices and prototypes.
